One pay period, with numbers.

A 3-provider Open Dental practice. Pay period Mar 1–15. Follow the dollars from the export to the paycheck error — then to the fix.

01 · Origin

Open Dental Provider Payroll export

Production totals look clean. They are not paycheck-ready — labs and write-off caps still live in the contract, not in the export.

Dr. Patel Production $24,057 raw
Dr. Nguyen Production $20,457 · labs in OD $2,650 raw
Hyg. Morales Production $9,227 · write-offs $480 raw
02 · The Friday spreadsheet

Contracts applied by hand — two silent mistakes

Patel is fine. Nguyen’s lab invoice is $3,100 (OD showed $2,650). Morales’s contract caps write-off deductions at $200 — the sheet deducted all $480.

Dr. Patel · 35% after labs $24,057 × 35% $8,420
Dr. Nguyen · used OD labs $2,650 ($20,457 − $2,650) × 35% $6,232
Hyg. Morales · deducted all write-offs ($9,227 − $480) × 33% $2,886

Paycheck drift this period: Nguyen ~$157 high · Morales ~$92 low — before anyone notices.
