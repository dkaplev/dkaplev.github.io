OpenClaw-Personal Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

OpenClaw-Personal is a private, single-user assistant operated by Dmitry Kaplev (dkaplev@gmail.com) for personal use only.

Data we access

With the owner’s Google consent, the app may access Gmail (read and send) and Google Calendar on the owner’s own Google account, solely to prepare briefings, reminders, and explicit send/create actions the owner approves.

How data is used

  • Processed on the owner’s private server.
  • Not sold, rented, or shared with advertisers.
  • Not used to build profiles for other people.

Storage

OAuth tokens and derived summaries are stored on the owner’s server under restricted file permissions. The owner can revoke access at any time in their Google Account security settings.
